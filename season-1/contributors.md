# Season 1 — DEATHCon 2026 contributors

Every accepted prompt is credited to its contributor in The Living Atlas.
Credits are permanent and appear here, in the repo, and in the workshop
materials.

**Conditions**

- The prompt must be original.
- The prompt must be accepted by LSI.
- Credits may be removed for plagiarism or abuse.

Credit uses the handle you submitted under. To be credited under a
different name, or not at all, say so in the workshop Discord.

## What credit is, and is not

| | |
|---|---|
| **The live board** | Your handle and best score, during the season. Resets between seasons. |
| **Contributor credit** | This page. Your handle on the accepted sample. Permanent. |
| **Research reporting** | Anonymized, always. Published findings carry no handles. |

Appearing on the board is not the same as being credited here — credit
follows acceptance, not submission.
